# 🏋️ My Gym + HIIT Workout Plan

> Based on Krish Ashok's video — build muscle (the body's glucose pharmacy), burn fat, live longer.
> **Goal:** Muscle building + Fat loss | **Equipment:** Dumbbells + Bench | **Time:** 15–20 mins/session

---

## ⏰ Best Time to Train

**Optimal window: 4 PM – 6 PM**

- Body temperature peaks → muscles warmer, stronger, more pliable
- Testosterone peaks → better muscle-building signal
- Cortisol (muscle-breakdown hormone) is lowest → net anabolic state
- Reaction time and coordination at their sharpest → better form, lower injury risk

> ⚠️ Hard rule: Never train within 2 hours of bedtime. It raises core temp + adrenaline and kills sleep quality — and sleep is when 80% of muscle repair happens.

## 📋 Circuit Design — How Every Session Works

### Format: Straight Sets with Controlled Rest

```
Warm-up     → 2 mins
Exercise 1  → Set 1 | rest 60s | Set 2 | rest 60s | Set 3
Exercise 2  → Set 1 | rest 60s | Set 2 | rest 60s | Set 3
Exercise 3  → Set 1 | rest 45s | Set 2 | rest 45s | Set 3
Cool-down   → 1 min
```

**Total: ~17–18 mins**

### Rep Tempo (apply to every exercise)

| Phase | Tempo | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Lowering (eccentric) | 3 seconds | Slow and controlled down |
| Pause at bottom | 1 second | Feel the stretch, don't bounce |
| Lifting (concentric) | 1 second | Push/pull with intent |

### Warm-up (same every day — 2 mins)
- 30 sec: Arm circles (forward + backward)
- 30 sec: Bodyweight squats × 10
- 30 sec: Hip circles
- 30 sec: Shoulder rolls + chest opener

### Cool-down (same every day — 1 min)
- 30 sec: Quad stretch (standing, hold bench if needed)
- 30 sec: Chest opener (clasp hands behind back, open chest up)

### Progressive Overload Rule
Every **2 weeks**, do one of:
- Add 1–2 reps to each set, OR
- Move up to the next dumbbell weight

## 🥩 Nutrition Reminder

- **Daily protein target:** 1.6g × your body weight in kg
- **Good sources:** Eggs, paneer, chicken, soy, curd, dal + rice combo
- **On HIIT days:** Eat a light snack 45–60 mins before (banana + peanuts, or curd + fruit)
- **Why it matters:** Without protein, the muscle-building signal from training doesn't convert into actual muscle

## 🧠 Key Principles (from Krish Ashok's video)

1. **Muscle = your body's pharmacy** — contracting muscle releases myokines that grow brain cells, reduce inflammation, burn fat
2. **Muscle = glucose parking lot** — 80% of blood glucose clears into skeletal muscle; more muscle = better blood sugar control = diabetes prevention
3. **60 mins/week of resistance training** = 33% reduction in all-cause mortality risk
4. **Abs are decoration, muscle is infrastructure** — focus on building muscle mass, not chasing visible abs
5. **Protein gap is real** — Indian diets are severely protein-deficient; you must actively fix this
